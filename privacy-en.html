X-Bar Privacy Policy

Version 0.5.0 · Updated October 23, 2026

Data collection by the developer

The X-Bar developer does not collect your account, posts or statistics or store them on developer servers. X-Bar includes no advertising, analytics or tracking SDK. It is a companion utility that reads and displays statistics on your Mac. Signing in to X and using X pages in the embedded browser are subject to X’s privacy policy.

Information processed on your Mac

X-Bar reads your account name, today’s impressions, post excerpts, impressions, likes and repost counts from the X analytics page you are signed into. These metrics and growth comparisons are processed in app memory and discarded when the app quits. Refresh and display preferences are stored on your Mac.

Web sign-in

X pages load in Apple WebKit. Cookies and web storage remain in the app’s own persistent data store on this Mac. X-Bar’s own code does not separately read or store passwords. X processes web requests for sign-in, analytics and its service according to its own policy. No X-Bar account or developer backend is used.

Optional Safari connection

After you enable the bundled Safari extension, allow x.com access and explicitly confirm the connection, the extension transfers only the x.com login cookies auth_token, ct0 and twid to X-Bar on this Mac. The temporary App Group transfer is encrypted with Apple CryptoKit. The app keeps the decryption key in memory. A one-time transfer request lasts 60 seconds and its temporary file is removed after reading. There is no time limit for preparing Safari or signing in. If a request expires, you can reconnect without signing in again. Accepted cookies remain in the app’s own WebKit store. Other-site cookies and saved passwords are not read.

Disconnecting and deletion

Disconnect in X-Bar settings removes the app’s WebKit data and current metrics and comparisons. Your Safari sign-in is preserved. Quitting X-Bar stops monitoring.
