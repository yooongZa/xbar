X-Bar Support

Check today’s X impressions and your posts’ performance from the Mac menu bar.

How to install ↗ · Follow the creator @yongZa_R ↗

Get started

  1. Launch X-Bar on macOS 14 or later.
  2. Choose Safari connection or Sign in in app. You need an X account that can access X Analytics.
  3. For Safari, enable the included extension in Safari Settings, allow access to x.com and confirm Connect to X-Bar in the extension. Sign in on X in Safari first if needed. For direct sign-in, use X’s sign-in page in the app. Use Safari if you normally sign in with Google.
  4. Setup finishes after your account and today’s impressions have been verified. Close the web window and click the menu bar number to see posts.

Updates and counts

Today / Last 2 days / Last 7 days refers to publication date. Post metrics are cumulative; the top-level impressions value is today’s total. Today’s value refreshes every 5, 10 or 30 minutes; post metrics are checked every 10 minutes. This is periodic monitoring.

X-Bar can refresh with its web window closed and Safari quit. Keep X-Bar running. Sleep, a lost connection, expired sign-in or a change to X may interrupt updates. Check the last updated time and use Refresh. Open the connection screen if sign-in or Analytics access needs attention.

Growth and the rainbow effect

Growth compares a post with the first value the app observed. The comparison resets when the app restarts or the date, time zone, account or selected period changes. The repost effect uses X-Bar’s own detection rules and can be disabled in settings.
